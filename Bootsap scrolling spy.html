Summer clothing is all about staying cool, comfortable, and expressive** when the weather heats up. Light fabrics like linen and cotton keep the air flowing, while bright colours, floral prints, and breezy silhouettes add a fun, sunny vibe to everyday outfits. Whether it’s a flowy sundress, a loose button‑up shirt, or airy shorts, summer pieces are designed to feel effortless and breathable, perfect for long days outside and warm evenings with friends. Winter clothing is all about **warmth, comfort, and protection** when the temperature drops. Instead of the light, airy fabrics you see in your summer‑clothing tab, winter style leans into thicker materials like wool, fleece, and padded insulation to keep heat close to your body. Coats, knitwear, scarves, and thermal layers become everyday essentials, and colours often shift toward deeper tones that feel cosy and seasonal. While summer outfits focus on breathability and flow, winter clothing is built for layering—mixing textures, adding outerwear, and creating looks that feel snug without sacrificing style. Autumn clothing leans into that cosy, in‑between season where the air turns crisp but not freezing. It’s all about **layers**—light knits, long‑sleeve tops, denim jackets, and soft cardigans that you can add or remove as the temperature shifts throughout the day. Colours usually warm up too, moving toward earthy tones like rust, mustard, olive, and deep browns that match the changing leaves. Fabrics get a little thicker than summer but still breathable, making pieces like flannel shirts, corduroy trousers, and ankle boots perfect for staying comfortable while embracing that warm, autumn vibe. Spring clothing is all about that fresh, light feeling when the world starts waking up again. As temperatures rise, layers become softer and more flexible—think **light cardigans, denim jackets, airy blouses, and flowy skirts**. Fabrics shift back toward breathable cotton and linen, but with just enough warmth for unpredictable breezy days. Colours brighten too, moving into pastels like lavender, mint, blush pink, and soft blue, echoing blooming flowers and longer daylight. Spring style is playful and transitional, perfect for mixing winter staples with summer favourites as the season slowly warms.
